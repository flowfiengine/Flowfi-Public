# Public roadmap

Where FlowFi is heading, in order. Directional, not a delivery schedule: we share each step when it is ready.

## Live now

- **FlowFi Pro:** Perps on SOL, ETH and BTC, the **Private room** (private USDC & USDT with **private sends** to other members, and gold, Bitcoin and stocks to buy), instant Early Radar alerts, a personal **Pro dashboard** (your positions, your alerts and how they ran, live signals), Pro charts, FLUX Pro, deeper history and higher limits. In three tiers (Pro, Pro+, Pro Elite) for $FLOWFI holders: hold $FLOWFI and Pro is yours.
- **Trading:** open to every member. Trading XP and fee cashback are recorded from the first trade.
- **LIVE Map V2** and a **Terminal for the whole Solana market**, with FlowFi Verified projects on top.
- **Studio launches and FlowFi Verified:** launch with SOL, USDC or tokenized stock pairs, or get an existing project verified.
- **5 languages:** English, French, Spanish, Indonesian and Simplified Chinese.
- **Network:** work profiles, follows, Insights, private messages, **FlowFi Pay** and **Bounties** with reviews and a public reputation record.
- **FLUX practice trades:** FLUX opens a $100 paper trade on FlowFi's picks, closes it by fixed rules, and every result is public.
- **Insight Contest:** hold a token, post your call; the best move after posting wins.
- **$FLOWFI:** live on FlowFi's own launchpad. Pay for FlowFi services with it (50% off for a limited time); every payment burns 50% and sends 50% to holders. **The FlowFi Wheel** pays the top 100 holders. **4.42% of supply is locked** into 2027.

## Next: a private portfolio

- **A lower FlowFi Pay fee** when you pay in $FLOWFI.

## Then: more in Pro
