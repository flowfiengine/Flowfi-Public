# Public roadmap

Where FlowFi is heading, in order. Directional, not a delivery schedule: we share each step when it is ready.

## Live now

- **FlowFi intelligence on Solana, BSC and Robinhood Chain:** the LIVE map with Early Radar and Validated, the FlowFi Firewall (11 published checks, including pulled liquidity and serial ruggers on EVM chains), and the public **Results** record by market.
- **FlowFi on iPhone:** on the App Store. **Android** is in closed testing.
- **FLUX:** FlowFi's assistant on every page, with voice, explaining the market in plain words.
- **Alerts:** Telegram and app push, around the clock.
- **FlowFi Pro:** for $FLOWFI holders, or by card with a 3-day free trial.
- **Intelligence website:** six languages, the four steps of FlowFi's intelligence and the Firewall checks, published.
- **Intelligence API:** early access.

## Next

- **Arbitrum and Base** on the LIVE map (in preview now).
- **Waking up:** older projects that capital flows back into, as their own listing next to Early Radar (in testing).
- **FlowFi on Android** for everyone, once Google's testing period completes.
- **Intelligence API** keys and plans.

## Then

- **Trading on more markets** inside the FlowFi Wallet.
- **FLUX trading** with take-profit and stop-loss, starting from public paper trades.

## Help shape FlowFi

Have an idea or found something confusing? Open an [Issue](https://github.com/flowfiengine/Flowfi-Public/issues) following [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

We don't publish internal implementation details, infrastructure, vendors, security controls, budgets or private schedules here.
