# Public roadmap

This is a **directional product roadmap**, not a delivery schedule or a promise that a feature is live. We share updates when there is a useful, public-facing milestone to report.

## Now — make discovery feel intuitive

- Refine the Attention Sphere exploration experience.
- Improve clarity, responsiveness, and usability of LIVE discovery.
- Make activity and momentum easier to interpret without overwhelming the user.

## Next — add more meaningful context

- Explore clearer explanations of attention and market activity.
- Continue shaping capital-activity context for the discovery experience.
- Gather early-user feedback and prioritize the most useful improvements.

## Later — expand the experience thoughtfully

- Explore personalized discovery and helpful notifications.
- Refine the experience across supported devices.
- Consider additional public-facing views as the product matures.

## Help shape FlowFi

Have a product idea or usability suggestion? Open a GitHub Issue following [our contribution guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md).

We intentionally do not publish internal implementation details, infrastructure, vendor configurations, private schedules, security controls, budgets, or unreleased technical work here.
