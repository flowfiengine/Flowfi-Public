# Public roadmap

Where FlowFi is heading, in order. Directional, not a delivery schedule: we share each step when it is ready.

## Live now

- **FlowFi Pro:** Perps on SOL, ETH and BTC, a seat in the Trading Beta at a 0.50% fee, the **Private room** (private USDC & USDT with **private sends** to other members, and gold, Bitcoin and stocks to buy), instant Early Radar alerts, a personal **Pro dashboard** (your positions, your alerts and how they ran, live signals), Pro charts, FLUX Pro, deeper history and higher limits. 3 days free, and payable from any Solana wallet.
- **Trading beta:** open to the top 25 of the Early Network leaderboard and to every Pro member. Trading XP and fee cashback are recorded from the first trade.
- **Network:** work profiles, follows, Insights, private messages, **FlowFi Pay** and **Bounties** with reviews and a public reputation record.
- **FLUX practice trades:** FLUX opens a $100 paper trade on FlowFi's picks, closes it by fixed rules, and every result is public.
- **Insight Contest:** hold a token, post your call; the best move after posting wins. The first one runs until November 5.
- **$FLOWFI:** live on FlowFi's own launchpad. Pay for FlowFi services with it (20% off Pro); every payment burns 50% and sends 50% to holders, paid hourly. **4.42% of supply is locked** into 2027.

## Next: Studio launches and a private portfolio

- **Private gold, Bitcoin and stocks** in the Private room.
- **Studio launches:** $19 per launch, review before you pay, the FlowFi Verified badge, a project page and launch protection. Creators keep 100% of the creator reward on every trade.
- **A lower FlowFi Pay fee** when you pay in $FLOWFI.

## Then: more in Pro

- **Take profit and stop loss on token trades**, limit orders and protected trading.
- **FLUX trading agents** and an **iOS app**.
