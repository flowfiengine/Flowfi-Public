# Introducing FlowFi Updates

**Published:** October 11, 2026  
**Category:** Platform  
**Availability:** Live

FlowFi now has a dedicated place for meaningful product improvements. The **Updates** page brings release announcements together in one easy-to-find feed, while a notification bell in the website header helps visitors spot newly published updates.

## What's changed

- Visit [FlowFi Updates](https://flowfiengine.app/updates) to read product announcements.
- Use the notification bell in the website header to open a compact summary of the latest updates.
- A small indicator highlights new announcements on the current device.

## Why it matters

FlowFi is evolving. This makes it easier to see what has changed without losing focus on the LIVE research experience.

Only reviewed announcements are published. A feature described as **Live** must be available in production; development work and plans are labeled separately.
