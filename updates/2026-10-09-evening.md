# FlowFi development update — October 9, evening

We're improving how FlowFi turns fast-moving market activity into understandable research.

## What changed

- **LIVE and Social Intelligence:** project connections distinguish evidence still being gathered from stronger observations. Supporting context is easier to inspect, and missing coverage isn't treated as proof of no activity.
- **Results:** project lookup and recorded discovery evidence are easier to explore before reviewing historical outcomes. Outcome comparisons include unresolved records and clear limitations.
- **Mobile and Terminal:** improved chart navigation, Quick Mode, accessible side panels, and the experience when returning to FlowFi.
- **Studio:** an illustrative preview of future intelligence and creative workflows. The examples are concepts, not a live content-generation or publishing service.

## What remains in progress

FlowFi LIVE and Results are evolving research experiences, not guarantees of market performance. Social observation coverage is selective. Trading execution is still under controlled testing, not broadly available, and this update is not financial advice.

**Our approach:** Intelligence in the back. Clarity in the front. Proof before confirmation.
